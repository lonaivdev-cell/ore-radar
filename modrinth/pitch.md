# Ore Radar: short descriptions

**Modrinth summary** (also in `project.json`; Modrinth allows 256 characters, this is 234):

> Pick an ore and the radar finds the nearest vein, then leads you to it: outlines through the
> stone, an arrow with the distance, a beep that speeds up as you close in. Light and
> server-controlled, for Forge, NeoForge, Fabric and Paper.

**GitHub "About"** (set on the repository):

> A handheld radar that finds the nearest ore and leads you to it with see-through outlines, an
> arrow and a beep. Forge and Fabric 1.20.1, NeoForge and Fabric 1.21.1, and a Paper plugin for
> players without mods.

**To share** (a message to friends, a forum post, a server announcement):

> Ore Radar is a handheld radar for Minecraft. Choose what you are looking for, from iron to
> ancient debris to a modded ore, and it finds the nearest one and guides you there with
> see-through outlines of the vein, an arrow showing the distance and height, and a beep that
> speeds up as you get closer. The server does the scanning on a small fixed budget and decides
> what can be tracked, so it stays light and fair. It runs on Forge and Fabric 1.20.1, NeoForge and
> Fabric 1.21.1, and as a Paper plugin that works for players with no mods at all.
